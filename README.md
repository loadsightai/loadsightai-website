# 🚚 LoadSight AI

## Know Every Load.

LoadSight AI is an AI-powered trailer intelligence platform built for trucking, agriculture, and bulk hauling.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

LoadSight AI uses artificial intelligence and computer vision to help drivers and fleet managers:

- 🌽 Estimate grain trailer fill levels
- 📦 Detect freight and available trailer space
- 🚛 Track trucks and trailers
- 📍 View GPS locations
- ⏱ Monitor driver hours and miles
- 📊 Improve fleet efficiency
